## Description:

But it's been "coming soon" for over a year now, and we've got no contact, no traces of the guy, and no more patience.

Fortunately, someone managed to find an "early access" port to one of his devices, if you know what I mean.
Un-fortunately, it seems that this port leads to some "all-new, totally one-of-a-kind computer architecture simulator sandbox thing" he'd been experimenting with.
I guess that explains all the delays.
It's weird, but it's the only opening we have. Maybe we can leave him a surprise message there.
