# Challenge: BabyRF
### Author: Suvoni

I made a nice flag for our beginner challenge but I accidentally dropped it and it broke into pieces. I collected them all into this cozy little .wav file for you to find!

Note: The flag follows the `L3AK{}` format, consists of 6 parts, and is case-sensitive. The ordering of the parts should be clear once you get all 6, but you may open a ticket if you encounter issues. The fourth part is all-uppercase.
