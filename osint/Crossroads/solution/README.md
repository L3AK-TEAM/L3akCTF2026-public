# Crossroads Solution
### Author: Eternal

A beginner-friendly challenge which places us in the middle of a rural intersection on a bright sunny day. We can easily determine the location using the road sign (``Trail Creek Rd``) and the election sign (``Idaho Senate``, clearly indicating the state of Idaho in the USA). A quick Google search finds the exact road, and the exact intersection can be found after a brief scan along the road.
