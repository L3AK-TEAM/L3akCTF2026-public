# Dry Run Solution
### Authors: OnyxCinder, Suvoni

We find ourselves in an old gas station. Visually, it appears abandoned or closed down due to the lack of people and general state of disarray. Cyrillic lettering on nearby objects indicates this could be Russia or a nearby country. The most relevant information to glean is that (1) the location is on the side of a rural highway, (2) the characters A3C-60 on the front (АЗС = автозаправочная станция, meaning gas station / filling station number 60), and (3) the fact that this is likely a defcunt (out-of-business) gas station, probably a small local one which went out of business.

After doing a lot of OSINT research on gas stations labeled A3C-60, we come across our target location [here](https://www.petrolplus.ru/about-company/news/novye-139-azs-prinimayut-nashi-karty/) and [here](http://www.ukn.ru/customers/russia.xls) (line 356).
