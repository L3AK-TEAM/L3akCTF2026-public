# Cherry Blossoms
### Authors: Suvoni, OnyxCinder

Imagine yourself amidst a calm and peaceful farmland, the air filled with the sweet smell of cherry blossoms in full bloom and the gentle sound of a trickling stream nearby. A secluded place where families live in humble harmony with nature, where countless memories are made beneath the springtime rain, the summer sun, and the winter snow. A place that reminds us of home, somewhere that never leaves us no matter where we go in life. 

When we find these beautiful places which we cherish dearly, we find a piece of ourselves as well.
